# Mobile Byte Sensei

A software product company from India. We build, publish and run our own products, on Android, iOS, the web and desktop.

## Products

- **[ReelExport](https://reelexport.com)**: an AI video studio, from a topic to a captioned, voiced reel
- **[Mood Movies](https://moodmovies.in)**: find a film by mood and see where to watch it (in development)
- **[PayCraft](https://paycraft.mobilebytesensei.com)**: billing for Kotlin Multiplatform apps ([open source](https://github.com/MobileByteLabs/PayCraft))
- **[ReelsDownloader](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sensei.social)**: 50,000+ downloads on Google Play
- **[MBS Galaxy](https://mbsgalaxy.com)**: our puzzle and board games, playable in the browser
